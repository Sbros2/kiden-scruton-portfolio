This project explores the stabilization of a two-wheel cart using contra-rotating gyroscopic flywheels, inspired by the Brennan monorail concept. It combines dynamic modeling, control logic, simulation work, and hands-on hardware iteration, including a semi-functional prototype, PID-based system thinking, and analysis of real design shortfalls such as excessive flywheel weight and motor control limitations.
